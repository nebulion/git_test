10% DISCOUNT FOR RESIDENTS OF THE BARCELONA METROPOLITAN AREA

If you are a resident of the Barcelona metropolitan area, you can purchase your general full festival ticket with a 10% discount by filling in the form below.

To process your request, you must attach your municipal register certificate proving that you live in the Barcelona metropolitan area.

⚠️ Other documents, such as bills and contracts, will not be accepted.

Once we’ve verified that the documentation is correct, you’ll receive an email with your discount code.

On the form, you’ll need to select whether you prefer to pay in instalments on AccessTicket or in a single payment on Fever.


*Discounts on full festival tickets are not cumulative or retroactive.

MINORS

Everyone, regardless of age, must have their own ticket to enter the festival.

For children aged 0 to 12, access is free on AccessTicket, where you must have a general full festival ticket for the accompanying adult registered in order to make a booking on this link.

For those aged 13 to 17, reduced-price tickets are available on Fever here.

Children aged 15 and under must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian to enter the festival. Under no circumstances will access be permitted with a third party.

ACCESSIBLE TICKET

If you are a person with reduced mobility or special needs, please fill in this form so that we can assess your case. Once we have reviewed your case, we will contact you as soon as possible and respect the price of the ticket at the time you requested it.

If you have any questions about purchasing your ticket, please write to accessibility@primaverasound.com
