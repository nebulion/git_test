Primavera en la Ciudad Buenos Aires

Primavera Sound Buenos Aires empieza antes: Llega Club Primavera

Primavera Sound Barcelona celebrates its unmistakable essence with an electrifying line-up for its 25th edition

Primavera Sound Barcelona 2026 kicks off its 24th edition today with an unparalleled line-up and a sold-out festival

June 1, 2026

T

he official version is that a new Primavera Sound Barcelona begins today, though experience has shown that this festival operates according to its own temporal logic. In reality, this edition, which is about to kick off, has already begun many times over the last twelve months: as many times as each attendee has imagined in their head what it will be like. How they will experience it. What will happen. What the best week of the year will sound like. But shouldn’t all that be perfectly clear by now at a festival that’s been running for a quarter of a century? Yes… and no. On the eve of its 24th edition, Primavera Sound is as much a collection of small traditions built by the community as it is a door thrown wide open for surprises to slip in.

You’ll need to keep your eyes and ears wide open, because the comforting familiarity and the dazzlingly unexpected may spring out at any moment of this week, which will feature a total of 330 shows, once again placing the city at the centre of the global music map. Because this is all about Primavera Sound, but also about Barcelona, which since 2001 has hosted, with the festival, countless performances that would otherwise never have taken place. From today until next Sunday, Primavera Sound once again plays its role in creating unforgettable memories, with over 290,000 attendees as direct witnesses.

Festivalgoers from 145 countries are already gathering around a distinctive line-up that screams ‘Primavera!’ in every aspect. A musical extravaganza that is only possible here, in both form and substance, which received the enthusiastic support of the festival community from the very start: tickets have been sold out for almost four months.

This second consecutive sold-out festival continues to establish Primavera Sound Barcelona as an annual gathering of people who come together spontaneously around something they love. Something of incalculable value in the current global scenario. In front of them all, and also in front of everyone who will be looking towards Barcelona from all around the world this week, extraordinary things are about to start happening any minute now.

THE VENUES WELCOME YOU. Once again, to think of Primavera Sound as merely what happens on the Parc del Fòrum site over three days would be to miss a large part of the picture. Starting this very afternoon, Primavera a la Ciutat will kick off the week with eight venues and spaces across Barcelona (Sala Apolo, La (2) de Apolo, Razzmatazz, Paral·lel 62, LAUT, La Nau, Les Enfants Brillants and the CCCB) directly involved in the festival: 91 performances spread across Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday that are much more than just a side event. The unusual indoor concerts by Geese, Mogwai and Blood Orange. The reunion with Santiago Motorizado, Primavera Sound’s ambassador in Latin America. The parties hosted by the XL Recordings and dh2 labels. The opportunity to discover such unique acts as flamenco guitarist Yerai Cortés, the cult apocalyptic folk group Current 93, the existentialist rapper billy woods, and Konono No. 1, legends of the Congotronics sound. Without a doubt, Primavera a la Ciutat also defines the festival’s distinctive character.

THE OPENING DAY. The Parc del Fòrum, home to Primavera Sound since 2005, will take centre stage on Wednesday, when concerts by Wet Leg, Guitarricadelafuente, Yard Act and Ouineta will fill the site with music for the first time during the Opening Day by Estrella Damm. A day of free performances to bring the city and the festival together from just after six in the evening until past eleven at night. And with more enthusiasm than ever: all tickets were reserved in record time. One of those rituals that make up the unique character of Primavera Sound. Those returning to the festival don’t miss a beat, and first-timers embrace it instantly because some things don’t need any explanation.

WHERE ELSE? If Wednesday’s concerts at the Parc del Fòrum are a bit like dipping your toe in the pool to check the water temperature, the big plunge comes on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, with the site running at full capacity. Three days headlined by The Cure, Doja Cat, Gorillaz, The xx, Addison Rae, Massive Attack and my bloody valentine, showcasing the festival’s cross-genre identity. Distorted rock, pure pop, fearless experimentation, urban sounds and cutting-edge electronica coexisting as is only possible here– where else? Seven names that show not only the journey the festival has taken to get here, but also its role as a musical chronicler of its time and place, renewing itself year after year. Whilst The Cure, Gorillaz, The xx and my bloody valentine will return to the festival after having left their mark on it in the past, Massive Attack will finally link their history to that of Primavera Sound. For their part, the performances by Doja Cat and Addison Rae will be their debut in Spain.

AS IMAGINED… However many surprises always find a way to creep into Primavera Sound, several certainties have already been determined in the narrative of this 24th edition before it even begins. This is sure to be the festival of PinkPantheress, who has brough about a re-evaluation of the concept of Britpop with the release of Fancy That. And of course that of Bad Gyal, a prophet in her own land. Of Geese, the band of the moment, led by Cameron Winter, who will also perform solo. Of Skrillex, Oklou and Peggy Gou. Of lo-fi idol Mac DeMarco, an old friend of the festival. Ethel Cain, Little Simz and Father John Misty. The Irish band KNEECAP, a most welcome addition to Primavera Sound for the second year running. Big Thief, Dijon, rusowsky, Slowdive, Alex G, JADE and Blood Orange. There are several things that will unfold exactly as one has imagined them.

… AND THE UNEXPECTED. Never, ever underestimate the power of persuasion of the Parc del Fòrum and its 18 stages when it comes to convincing you to do something you hadn’t planned. It doesn’t matter how much experience you’ve already got from previous editions of the festival: that’s precisely why you know better than anyone that musical stimuli can come from any direction, causing you to change your route in real time. It’s the magnetic power of a site that, over the course of Thursday, Friday and Saturday alone, will host 231 performances. You can jump from the seats of the Auditori Rockdelux—which returns to the Primavera Sound line-up after a year’s hiatus—to the CUPRA Pulse dance floor, which on Saturday will feature none other than Skrillex presenting a line-up that for now remains a complete mystery. You can delve into the sonic depths of The Levi’s Warehouse or head to the festival’s seafront promenade, where the Disney stage awaits with performances by Absolutely and Martin with a giant Mickey Mouse figure presiding over it all. You can catch up with your friends after singing along to the Catalan choruses of Renaldo & Clara and listen to them telling you they saw a black metal band called Agriculture and a Scottish bagpiper called Brìghde Chaimbeul. You can be part of Cara Delevingne’s new musical venture, of the cinematic story of Beverly Glenn-Copeland and Elizabeth Copeland, and of the mystique surrounding the pioneering techno collective Underground Resistance. You can embrace the rhythm as if you were in Brazil with xavisphone, in the Maghreb with Guedra Guedra or in Puerto Rico with DJ Playero. You can discover new stars like fakemink, 2hollis, Ninajirachi, iza tkm and The New Eves so that your future self will owe you one. In short, you can experience Primavera Sound Barcelona with everything that entails.

A CONSCIOUS FESTIVAL. Of course, all this ambition doesn’t stop at the music. Aware of its social and environmental context, Primavera Sound works year after year to continue expanding the sustainability framework within which it takes place and to maximise the return on its activities. The ‘Nobody is Normal’ protocol against sexual assault and harassment will ensure the festival is celebrated as a community. The commitment to using batteries to power most stages will boost energy efficiency. Various accessibility measures will make the experience easier for people with reduced mobility or neurodivergent people. The collaboration with Top Manta Barcelona will bring the reality and work of the Popular Union of Street Vendors in the city closer to the audience at the Parc del Fòrum. The agreement with D’Ins Gastronomía Inclusiva will enable almost 100 people in vulnerable situations to work in the canteen where festival staff refuel. The installation of free water fountains in partnership with Aigües de Barcelona will reduce plastic waste. The partnership with Dinder Club, Som Fundació and Fundació Superacció will ensure that over a hundred people with disabilities or at risk of social exclusion attend the festival. Because at Primavera Sound Barcelona, important things don’t only happen on stage.

ONE LAST DANCE. Just a few hours after Peggy Gou’s set on the CUPRA stage in the early hours of Saturday morning serves as the official closure of the three main days of this 24th edition, the Primavera Bits by Occident closing party on 7th June will offer one last dance with Carl Cox, Joseph Capriati, BLOND:ISH and Greta (with the last tickets specifically for this party still on sale). Meanwhile, as the Parc del Fòrum becomes a huge open-air dance floor from the early hours of Sunday afternoon until 11 pm, Primavera a la Ciutat will once again shine a spotlight on Barcelona’s venues with concerts by Black Country, New Road, Ela Minus, Six Sex, Cass McCombs and Bassvictim. There will still be so much to do on Sunday that even Primavera Sound Barcelona 2026 will end up stealing a few hours from the Monday before bidding a final farewell.

LIVE CONNECTION WITH THE FESTIVAL. For the fifth consecutive year, Amazon Music will broadcast a selection of almost forty headline concerts live and exclusively via Prime Video, the Amazon Music app and its official Twitch channels. On each of the three main days, from 19:00 CEST, the official Amazon Music stream will connect with the Parc del Fòrum so that the performances by The Cure, Gorillaz, Doja Cat, The xx, Addison Rae, Bad Gyal, Skrillex, Mac DeMarco, Geese, Ethel Cain, Little Simz, Big Thief, Oklou, Alex G, Father John Misty, Wet Leg, rusowsky, Amaarae, Guitarricadelafuente, Men I Trust, TV Girl, MARINA, JADE, Role Model, Ralphie Choo, 2hollis, Knocked Loose, Ravyn Lenae, Viagra Boys, Sudan Archives, Buscabulla, Jimena Amarillo, Grace Ives, Gisela João, Baxter Dury, Pavvla and Bestia Bebé will also echo around the world. Also, iCat and Betevé will broadcast live the concerts from the Inaugural Day by Estrella Damm and the Radio Primavera Sound studio at the festival will offer another way for the public to connect closely with some of their favourite artists.

THE INDUSTRY HAS ITS SAY. We are not just saying that the entire music industry regards Primavera Sound Barcelona as one of the must-attend events on the international calendar. This is a reality that is palpable at Primavera Pro, the festival’s professional gathering, which will bring together over 3,000 accredited professionals from 50 countries for its 17th edition. With the Centre de Cultura Contemporània de Barcelona (CCCB) as its headquarters, Primavera Pro will host a wide-ranging programme of conferences and workshops, as well as around twenty free daytime concerts as part of Primavera a la Ciutat, from Wednesday 3rd to Friday 5th. Artists, managers, creative directors, music supervisors and journalists will take the floor to analyse the current state of the sector and look ahead to its future.

Primavera Sound Barcelona 2026 is supported by Estrella Damm, Revolut, CUPRA and Occident as key strategic partners. Their support makes it possible to continue improving the festival, in which we believe so strongly, both musically and in everything that surrounds it. They are joined by the essential collaboration of Levi’s, Schwarzkopf, Jameson, Aperol, Zalando, adidas, Vueling, azulmarino, Meller, Cantabria Labs, Magnum and Red Bull, as well as Fever as the official point of sale. Many thanks to everyone for your faith in Primavera Sound.
