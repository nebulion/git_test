Primavera Sound Buenos Aires empieza antes: Llega Club Primavera
Primavera Sound Barcelona celebrates its unmistakable essence with an electrifying line-up for its 25th edition
New additions to the Parc del Fòrum: Primavera Sound Barcelona 2026 expands even further with the addition of five new stages

May 29, 2026

L

evi’s is joining Primavera Sound Barcelona for the second year running and is doing so in a big way. In addition to The Levi’s Warehouse, which will continue to be the home to the line-up’s most elusive sounds for yet another edition, its collaboration with the festival will include two further stages, the line-ups for which are now being revealed: The Levi’s Plaza and The 501 Club curated by COLORSxSTUDIOS. So, the line-up for the 24th edition of Primavera Sound across its three main days expands even further with the support of Levi’s.

On the one hand, Babymorocco, Barry B, CHÉRI, Cobrah, European Vampire, Isabella Lovestory, Natalia Lacunza, Rose Gray and Teo Planell will perform at The Levi’s Plaza. On the other, The 501 Club curated by COLORSxSTUDIOS – marking the debut of the popular German online performance platform at the festival – will host concerts by Bel Cobain, Haich Ber Na, haloplus+, Korashe, Léa Sen, Rian Brazil, Sade Olutola, waterbaby and Ydegirl.

An open-air stage at one end of the site and another indoor space, tucked away in one of those little corners at Primavera Sound that you should never overlook, to welcome new residents to the Parc del Fòrum. From Babymorocco’s sexy party to Rose Gray’s retro soul; from Ydegirl’s Copenhagen-made pop to the anthems of Barry B, who will be performing twice at the festival; from Honduran artist Isabella Lovestory, an expert in advanced perreo, to British singer Léa Sen, a guest vocalist on the latest albums by Sampha, Vegyn and Joy Orbison. From 4th to 6th June, the collaboration between Levi’s and Primavera Sound will take centre stage on three of the seventeen stages at the Parc del Fòrum, but there’s more.

The Backstage by Schwarzkopf will host performances by Annahstasia, jasmine.4.t, LUSILLON and spill tab, with several of them playing their second set of the week. The Barcelona Sona by Estrella Damm stage will come alive with concerts and sets by Akazie, Ale Hope, Chica Acosta, Hadren, Ivy Barkakati, Jan, MA RA, Menine i Nicole.aiff, while at the Aperol Island of Joy stage, the meeting point for national talent, CORTE!, dani dicostas, Dharmacide, Gara Durán, Mourn, Ouineta, paco te quiero, Perfecto Miserable and SVSTO will come together, though there will also be time for live broadcasts of the Amiga date cuenta and Bombificadas podcasts.
